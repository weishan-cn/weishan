# Golden Decision Paths

Eight offline experience Contracts cover product comparison, travel, work, learning, finance-information risk, services, general decisions, and past-decision review. Each keeps Question First, evidence and risk visibility, user decision, predictable exit, and no more than three steps before user-confirmed Workspace creation.

Golden Paths do not execute business actions: they cannot create Workspaces automatically, save Archives automatically, select options, or open Providers.
